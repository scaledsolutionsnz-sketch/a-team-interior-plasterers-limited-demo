What We Do

Interior plastering services
built around your project.

Whether it's a brand-new home, a full renovation, or a targeted repair — we have the skills and experience to deliver a result you'll be happy with for years to come.

🏗️
New Build Plastering

Full interior plastering for new residential builds throughout Christchurch. We work closely with builders and project managers to keep your timeline on track.

🔨
Renovations & Alterations

Plastering for home renovations — from room additions and layout changes to full whole-home refurbs. Matched finishes, seamless joins, clean results.

✋
Gib Stopping & Finishing

Expert gib stopping to Level 4 and Level 5 standard. Whether you're painting or wallpapering, we deliver the surface quality your finish demands.

🪣
Skimming & Levelling

Skim coating over existing walls and ceilings to create a smooth, uniform surface — ideal for older homes or surfaces showing wear, damage or texture inconsistencies.

🧱
Texture & Plaster Coatings

Applied texture finishes for interior walls and ceilings — from fine sand to more textured effects. Great for feature walls and high-traffic areas requiring character and durability.

🔧
Repairs & Patching

Professional repair work for cracked, damaged, or water-affected plaster. We match existing textures and finishes so repairs blend seamlessly with the surrounding surface.
